Australian retail turnover forecasting

Python, pandas, statsmodels

SARIMA beat both alternatives in every state, cutting average error by up to 65% against a seasonal baseline.

Monthly retail turnover from the ABS for three states, cleaned in pandas. The series show strong annual seasonality and long-term growth, so I compared a seasonal naive baseline, Holt-Winters and SARIMA on a time-based train and test split, scored on MAE, RMSE and MAPE.

Australia trade trends

Tableau, Excel

Natural gas rose from 2% to nearly 40% of energy exports, while coal's share fell from about 85% to 55%.

Thirty-seven years of ABS trade data across ten product categories, shown three ways: dollar value, growth index and share of total. Built as Tableau dashboards and a story, with commentary on what drove each shift.

Aged care efficiency benchmarking

Python, SciPy, pandas, Excel

Five facilities set the benchmark, and the two least efficient needed about 40% more output to match their peers.

A capstone project with two aged care providers. We used Data Envelopment Analysis to compare how well each facility turned staffing into quality outcomes, adjusting for how complex each facility's residents were.

Group of six. I built the DEA model and code and the resident acuity index.
